# Basin water-year accounting

The Basins and River basins pages balance each of the eight major basins over a water year.

| Term | Source |
|---|---|
| Precipitation | gridMET basin-mean precipitation times basin area (1 mm over 1 km² is 0.8107 AF) |
| Outflow | Sum of the outlet gauges where the basin's rivers leave the state (see the outlet list in the repository) |
| Exports and imports | Transbasin diversions out of and into the basin |
| Storage change | Change in the major reservoirs from September 30 to September 30 |

A year counts only if every outlet gauge has at least 330 days of data and the climate record is complete.

**Runoff ratio** is outflow plus exports minus imports, over precipitation volume. It is the share of precipitation that leaves as surface water. The rest evaporates, is used by plants and people in the basin, or recharges groundwater. It is left blank where imports exceed outflow plus exports.

**The hundred drops** on the public pages split a basin's or the state's precipitation into outflow, transfers across the Continental Divide, and the remainder (used or evaporated in the state), rounded to whole drops by the largest remainder method so they add to 100.

This accounting is approximate. Outflow gauges miss some small ungauged streams, storage change covers only the mapped reservoirs, and consumptive use is the remainder rather than a measurement. The [irrigation chapters](08-irrigation-cu.md) measure that use directly for one basin.
